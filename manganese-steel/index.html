MNxtra, for the zones that wear first.

Every crusher has places where liners fail before the rest. MNxtra is the manganese grade our metallurgists developed for those high wear zones, where conventional grades reach their limits.

Send us the worn part or its wear profile. We’ll tell you honestly whether MNxtra, or another grade, will last longer.

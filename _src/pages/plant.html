The process

Nine steps. No shortcuts.

01 · METHOD

Method design

Feeding and gating designed in-house for every new part.

02 · PATTERN

Pattern shop

Patterns made and checked against the drawing.

03 · MOULDING

No-bake moulding

20 t/h Alphaset continuous mixer, mould pre-heater, 3 t/h core mixer.

04 · MELTING

Melting

Two 3,000 kg induction crucibles. Every heat tested by spectrometer before pouring.

05 · POURING

Pouring

Bottom-pour ladles for clean metal into the mould.

06 · HEAT TREATMENT

Solution treat & quench

Two 10 t gas-fired box furnaces with water quenching.

07 · FETTLING

Fettling & finishing

Air arc gouging, 96″ table shot blasting, qualified welders and grinders.

08 · MACHINING

Machining

7 VTLs up to 2,300 mm and a plano miller with a 4,000 mm table.

09 · INSPECTION

Inspection & dispatch

Dimensions checked with gauges, jigs and fixtures for fit at site.

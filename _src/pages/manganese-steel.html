How it works

Tough when new. Hard where it matters.

Austenitic manganese steel leaves the water quench at about 190–240 HB. Under the repeated impact of crushing, the working face hardens to above 500 HB while the core stays tough. That combination is why it has been the standard material for crusher liners for more than a century.

It also has a limit: it needs impact to harden. Choosing the right grade for your crusher and your rock decides how long it lasts.

Metallurgy that drives performance

MNX

Built for the zones that wear first.

Every crusher has places where liners fail before the rest: the feed zone, the parallel zone, the spot the rock hits hardest. MNX is the manganese grade our metallurgists developed for those high-wear zones, where conventional grades reach their limits.

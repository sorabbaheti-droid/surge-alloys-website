The plan

  • Use less

    Energy audits across the plant, with their recommendations put into practice.

  • Generate our own

    Solar on site. Current rules allow about 25% of our projected demand.

  • Buy the rest green

    About 75% of our electricity must come from green sources by 2030, including renewable energy certificates.

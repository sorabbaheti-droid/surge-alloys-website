Choosing the grade

Poured to ASTM A128, IS 276, EN 10349 or your specification. We confirm the grade for your duty with every quotation.

GradeWhat it isWhere it works
Mn 14Standard austenitic manganese steel, about 12 to 14% Mn.General crushing: jaws, cones and impact parts in most rock.
Mn 18Higher manganese for faster, deeper work hardening.Harder, more abrasive rock and heavier impact.

How to order

Three steps from your worn part to a new set.

  1. Send us the part

    A drawing, a worn sample, or photographs with the main dimensions. We can arrange a 3D scan.

  2. We recommend and quote

    Our application specialist matches the grade to your rock and your crusher, then sends a quotation.

  3. We cast, check and dispatch

    Poured, heat treated, machined where the drawing asks, and gauged before it leaves.

Questions buyers ask

Can you make roll crusher shells without a drawing?

Yes. Send a worn sample, or photographs with the main dimensions. We can also arrange a 3D scan and rebuild the geometry from it.

Which grade should I choose?

It depends on your rock and how hard the crusher hits it. For roll crusher shells we pour Mn 14, Mn 18, or the grade you specify, and we confirm the choice with every quotation.

How large can you cast?

Single pieces up to 4.5 tonnes.

Do you machine the parts?

Yes. Parts are supplied as cast or machined to your drawing. Our plant has 7 vertical lathes, the largest turning 2,300 mm.

How do you check quality?

The chemistry of every heat is tested on a spectrometer before pouring. Dimensions are checked with gauges, jigs and fixtures before dispatch.

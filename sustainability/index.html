Sustainability · ESG

−42% by 2030.

Our science-based target, validated through the SBTi route for small and medium-sized enterprises: a 42% cut in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030, against our 2021 baseline. We also measure and work to reduce our Scope 3 emissions.

Where our emissions come from

Most of it is electricity.

20%
80%
Scope 1 · fuelsScope 2 · electricity

Scope 1 (20%): LPG for heat treatment, scrap cutting, ladle pre-heating and maintenance, and diesel for the DG set and the sand-handling loader.

Scope 2 (80%): the electricity that runs our induction furnaces and the rest of the plant.

How we get there

The plan.

01 · EFFICIENCY

Use less

Energy audits across the plant, with the actions they recommend put into practice.

02 · SOLAR

Generate our own

We generate electricity in-house, led by solar. Current rules let us install solar to about 25% of our projected demand.

03 · GREEN POWER

Buy the rest green

To meet 2030, about 75% of our electricity must come from green sources, including renewable energy certificates.

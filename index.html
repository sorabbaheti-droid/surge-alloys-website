  • Are your wear parts failing earlier than expected?

    The grade may not suit your rock or your crusher’s impact. We match the manganese grade to the duty.

    Parts that last longer and fit first time mean fewer stoppages.

It gets harder where it’s hit.

Manganese steel leaves our water quench soft and tough. Under the blows of crushing, its working face hardens while the core stays tough, so it resists gouging without turning brittle.

  • As quenched 190 to 240 HB
  • Working face in service 500+ HB
  • Core stays tough

Five grades. One for your rock.

Including MNxtra, our own grade for the zones that wear first.

GradeWhat it isWhere it works
Mn 14Standard austenitic manganese steel, about 12 to 14% Mn.General crushing: jaws, cones and impact parts in most rock.
Mn 18Higher manganese for faster, deeper work hardening.Harder, more abrasive rock and heavier impact.
Mn 22The highest-manganese type, for the toughest duty.Severe impact and heavy sections.
Cr-Mo alloyedManganese steel with chromium and molybdenum additions.Where liners spread or deform in service, and for abrasive feed.
MNxtraOur own manganese grade, custom-developed by our metallurgists.High-wear zones where conventional manganese grades reach their limits.

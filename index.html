It gets harder where it’s hit.

Manganese steel leaves our quench tough. Under the blows of crushing, its working face hardens to above 500 HB while the core stays tough.

We pour 14, 18 and 22% Mn, chromium-molybdenum alloyed grades, and MNxtra, our own grade for the zones that wear first.
